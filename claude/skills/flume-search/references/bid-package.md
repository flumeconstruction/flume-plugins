# The bid package

For a general contractor or a tile or flooring subcontractor bidding with a spec list or a
finish schedule in hand: "help me bid on this project", "price this finish schedule for our
bid", "put together a bid package". SKILL.md's rules hold. This file adds what a bid needs.

## What a bid adds

- The up-front ask also covers the bid due date and whether to include alternates.
- Per line, the base pick matches the spec; the alternates are the line's other options.
- Check each instant quote's lead time and expiry date against the bid due date.
- Save the picks to a Flume project (one project card), then lay out the package below.

## Per line

- The spec as written (the reference) beside the base pick and its alternates: name, ships
  from with its flag, key specs as the product page prints them, delivered price per SF,
  the instant quote's total for the line's quantity, lead time, product link.
- The base pick's one-line reason, and for each alternate why it is offered, in plain words
  (cheaper, a shorter lead time, a closer look).

## Totals

- The base bid per category and overall, added up from the instant quotes.
- Alternates as separate add or deduct lines against the base, worked out from their
  instant quotes, so the bidder can carry value engineering options without changing the
  base.

## Clarifications

- Prices include goods, freight and duty.
- Prices exclude sales tax (shown separately), installation, setting materials and trims.
  Trims are not in Flume search: a trim line goes to Flume's sales team.
- Each product's minimum order.
- Lead time per option, against the project schedule.

## Validity

- The expiry date of every instant quote, beside the bid due date. When one expires before
  the bid date, say so and offer a new instant quote closer to the date.

## Open items

- Lines that need a photo or a data sheet.
- Lines handed to Flume's sales team (stone, wood, anything outside tile and vinyl), after
  the person agreed.
- Lines below a product's minimum.

## Scope

- The bidder's own markup, labor and other trades stay theirs: the package covers Flume's
  material only, and says so.
- Instant quotes for a bid count against the same daily limit as on the web.
