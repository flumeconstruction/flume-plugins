# The VE report (value engineering)

For a VE report, a value engineering summary, an options report or a comparison document
from a spec list. SKILL.md's rules hold (prices, totals, documents, pictures). The
must-haves come first; the rest is optional and bends to the data. Lay the document out your
own way.

## Must-haves

### One block per line

- The reference, as the person stated it: name or code, size, material, finish, look,
  where it goes, quantity. The picture the person gave, or the product image you searched
  with, as the reference image.
- The Flume options beside it, one to three (fewer when the rest look the same): the title
  as Flume printed it, ships from (the
  place and its flag from `get_product`), the specs as printed (labels verbatim), why it
  is offered in plain words, the price with its wording, the lead time, the product link,
  one picture by its label.
- One recommended pick, with its one-line reason.

### Totals

- Per category and overall, from the instant quotes, with savings against the reference
  where they help.

### Open items

- Lines that named no product ("needs a photo" or "send the data sheet"), lines for
  Flume's sales team, lines below a product's minimum, lines Flume returned nothing for.

## Optional, when the data allows

- Cover: project name, client, contact, address, date, from the person's words
  (`get_profile` gives only the connected account).
- Summary counts: lines analyzed, options sourced, categories covered (count your own
  calls).
- The three value messages, in these words:
  - For the developer: material cost known before the order, delivered to the project,
    with alternates that hold the spec.
  - For the designer: the specified look kept, with the specs beside every option, so the
    design intent survives value engineering.
  - For the contractor: lead times, minimums and delivered pricing per option, so the
    schedule and the bid hold.
- Next steps: samples on Flume's self-serve app or through the sales team; the order through
  Flume's sales team, by request from the chat; a fresh instant quote closer to the order
  date.

## Rows

- Color is not a row Flume prints: describe it from the person's reference, or leave it
  out.
- Prices: the as-low-as per SF and the instant quote's delivered figures only. No cost
  build-up, supplier price, markup or carrier.
